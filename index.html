Our Story

Made in the fields. Bottled with a promise.

किसान की मेहनत, आपके परिवार का स्वास्थ्य।

Jai Kishaan started with a simple frustration: it had become hard to find mustard oil that actually tasted like mustard oil. Most bottles on the shelf are refined, blended, or diluted for a longer shelf life — at the cost of the sharp aroma and pungency that Indian cooking depends on.

So we went back to the traditional Kachi Ghani method — a slow, single cold-press extraction that keeps the oil's natural colour, aroma and nutrients intact. Every batch is sourced from mustard farms, pressed without heat, and bottled without shortcuts.

  • Sourced directly from mustard farmers Fair prices, shorter supply chain, fresher seed stock.
  • Single cold-press extraction No heat, no solvents — just the traditional wooden ghani method.
  • Batch-tested for purity Every batch checked before it leaves the unit.
How It's Made

From mustard field to your kitchen shelf

Four honest steps. No refining, no shortcuts — the way mustard oil was always meant to be made.

01

Seed Selection

Bold, mature mustard seeds are sourced directly from partner farms and cleaned to remove chaff and impurities.

02

Kachi Ghani Pressing

Seeds are crushed slowly at low temperature in a traditional wooden ghani, preserving natural pungency and nutrients.

03

Natural Filtration

The oil is filtered mechanically — no chemical refining, bleaching, or deodorising involved.

04

Bottled Fresh

Sealed at the source in food-grade bottles, so what reaches your kitchen is what came out of the press.
